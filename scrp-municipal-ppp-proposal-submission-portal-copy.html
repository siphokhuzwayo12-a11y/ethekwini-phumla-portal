1 Executive Engagement & Snapshot

Following direct executive engagement with Phakamile Mbonambi (phakamile.mbonambi@durban.co.za) of the Executive Leadership of the Office of the City Manager, this document establishes the formal execution roadmap and technical concept for the Sovereign Circular Resource Park (SCRP).

The Sovereign Circular Resource Park (SCRP) is an integrated municipal resource recovery ecosystem designed to convert municipal waste streams and underutilized resources into productive economic assets. The framework brings together clean bio-solar generation, adaptive climate shields, circular waste recovery, and community economic development into a unified, scalable municipal innovation platform.

2 Strategic Alignment (Why eThekwini Municipality)

The SCRP framework addresses critical municipal waste infrastructure challenges and directly aligns with eThekwini Metropolitan Municipality priorities across six key vectors:

  • Waste Diversion Objectives: Accelerated diversion of organic, construction, demolition, and inert waste streams from municipal landfill systems.
  • Circular Economy Development: Transformation of municipal waste liabilities into productive economic assets and high-value materials.
  • Infrastructure Renewal: Deployment of resilient, decentralized energy, water, and material recovery infrastructure.
  • Climate Adaptation: Reduction of environmental burdens through localized resource circulation and clean energy generation.
  • Local Economic Development: Creation of sustainable enterprise opportunities across energy, agriculture, construction recovery, and circular economy sectors.
  • Skills Transfer & Inclusive Growth: Development of structured technical learning pathways for youth and emerging practitioners.

6 Core Operational, Environmental & Financial Pillars

A. Financial Mechanics & Revenue Capture

  • Direct Tipping Fees: Municipal revenue protection via R150-R300 per ton tariff structure.
  • Landfill Capital Preservation: Prevents R450M+ in municipal land acquisition and expansion costs.
  • Sustained Margins: Targets 75%-85% gross operating margins across local energy and mineral sales.

B. Engineering & Environmental Safeguards

  • Biosecurity Isolation: 4-Zone separation keeping clean agricultural production isolated upwind.
  • Thermal Pathogen Elimination: 55°C thermophilic digestion + 70°C pasteurization (60 min retention).
  • Zero-Cross Water Circuit: Hydrological segregation between industrial processing water and crops.

C. Universal Governance & Disability Inclusion

  • Accessibility Compliance: Zero-step access routes, 1,200mm doorways, height-adjusted workstations, and voice/visual diagnostics.
  • Targeted Workforce Inclusion: Minimum 10% direct operational staffing reserved for persons with disabilities with adaptive tools.

7 Capital Strategy, Phase Clarification & Ledger Structure

Important Notice — Long-Term Framework Clarification

The Sovereign Circular Resource Park is a long-term strategic infrastructure framework. Any capital allocations referenced within supporting technical documents relate exclusively to specific demonstrator phases and technical modules.

The commonly referenced R3.1 Million CAPEX Buffered Ledger applies strictly to the proposed Phase 1 Bio-Solar Engine Demonstrator and does not represent the full capital requirements of the complete Sovereign Circular Resource Park.

Future SCRP expansion modules—including Municipal Resource Recovery, Construction Waste Recovery, Green Mineral Integration, Agrivoltaic Expansion, and Regional Circular Economy Rollout—remain subject to technical studies, municipal review, PPP structuring (including target IDC 49% public equity participation), engineering design, and funding determination.

8 Universal Accessibility & Inclusion Engineering

Physical & Mobility Access

Zero-step access routes, 1,200mm thresholds, height-adjusted workstations, and mobility-inclusive infrastructure across all plant zones.
